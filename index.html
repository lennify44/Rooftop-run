Nine sections of pillars, beams, moving slabs, a wall run, a chimney, bounce pads and a slide roof above a city that isn't there anymore. Reach the gold roof. Each section hides a paper lantern where only a full, well-aimed jump reaches it. Long gaps need a jump from the very edge; before small targets, brake in the air. The last gap needs a slide jump.
